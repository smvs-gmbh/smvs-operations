      About this proposal

      This is the End-user view for the location involved in the alert. The later MAH view will share the case context but ask for upload and serialisation evidence. SMVS coordinates the investigation and decides the outcome.

      The alert type and operation-code context determine which checks you see. Existing evidence is reused; only missing information is requested.

      A3 prioritises pack comparison and technical checks. A2 focuses on the batch and upload question. A supported same-location A7 can be resolved by SMVS without asking you to complete a questionnaire.

      All cases are fictional. Packshot selection demonstrates the interaction only: the file contents are not read, uploaded or retained.
