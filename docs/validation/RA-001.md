Draft v0.2, 2026-11-17. Owner SMVS GmbH. Assessment basis is the local
validation candidate `d446f5d2d6913403da53ab8ce41df76f5e65ccfe` and the
release scope in [VP-001](../VP-001%20Validation%20Plan.md). The target
deployment and risk acceptance require confirmation by SMVS.

Assess the severity of a plausible failure and the likelihood before the
listed controls. Severity is 3 for an incorrect case decision, lost critical
evidence, serious disclosure or loss of service for a critical workflow,
2 for recoverable operational impact, and 1 for minor operational impact.
Likelihood is 3 when exposure is frequent, 2 when plausible in ordinary use
or change, and 1 when exceptional. Scores of 6–9 are High, 3–4 Medium and
1–2 Low. Likelihood ratings are preliminary judgement without measured
failure rates. Confirm residual risk after controls are verified in the
applicable release environment. Review the assessment when release scope,
configuration, interfaces or mitigations change.

## Control ownership and timely detection

For each release, review affected stakeholders and possible consequences for
medicines verification decisions, data and audit integrity, confidentiality,
interoperability, continuity and operational use. Link material impacts to a
risk ID. Record a short rationale where no further assessment is needed.

Proposed ownership is by role. Confirm a named owner at release review.
Use timely detection to select verification and monitoring. A warning or
manual review is effective only if it can act before the consequence occurs.
This judgement complements the initial score.

| Risks | Affected parties and proposed owner | Detection to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RA-01, RA-08, RA-10 | Participants and SMVS. System owner. | Reject cross-scope or expired access before disclosure, and verify that production cannot route to synthetic or unapproved features. |
| RA-02, RA-03, RA-13 | SMVS investigators and report reviewers. Data integration owner. | Show partial, stale or unmatched evidence before a reviewer draws a conclusion. |
| RA-04, RA-11 | SMVS and responsible NMVO. Investigation process owner. | Reject stale or out-of-scope changes before saving. For the planned Hub integration, demonstrate that an unconfirmed or partial Hub update leaves the Case open. |
| RA-05, RA-06, RA-09 | End Users, MAHs and SMVS. Participant workflow owner. | Verify that incorrect dates, changed recipients, unresolved links and uncertain deliveries are visible before follow-up or repeat delivery. |
| RA-07, RA-12 | SMVS staff and evidence reviewers. Operations system owner. | Detect a migration or restore discrepancy during rehearsal or post-deployment checks before the affected release is relied on. |
| RA-14 | Participants and SMVS staff. Service operations owner. | Make failed, delayed or backlogged processing visible in time for staff to act. Verify the operating response as well as recovery. |

The [traceability matrix](RTM-001.md) maps these risks to requirements and
candidate evidence. Record the verified control result, deviations, residual
severity and likelihood, and whether timely detection is demonstrated before
recording the residual-risk decision, approver and date. Where a control relies
on an operating procedure or staff guidance, verify that the procedure or
guidance is available and that the intended role can carry it out.
